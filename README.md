# 🚀 Mission-Marz

Welcome to **Mission-Marz** — a 3D Martian base-building and survival game built right in your web browser! 

Design and build your own human outpost on Mars, pick your landing site, manage your life support systems, and find out if your colony can survive 30 Sols on the Red Planet.

---

## ✨ What You Can Do

* **Explore Mars:** Choose from real NASA landing sites (like Jezero Crater or Utopia Planitia) on an interactive 3D planet globe.
* **Build Your Base:** Snap together living pods, solar panels, nuclear reactors, greenhouses, and rover garages on a 3D grid.
* **Keep Your Crew Alive:** Balance your power, water, oxygen, and food to make sure your astronauts make it through the mission.
* **Survive the Elements:** Face Martian dust storms, freezing nights, and solar flares during the 30-day simulation.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Three.js / WebGL** for the 3D graphics and animations
* **React & Vite** for a smooth user interface
* **NASA ECLSS Data** for realistic life-support and resource math
